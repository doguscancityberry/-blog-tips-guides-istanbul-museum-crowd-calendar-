Short answer

For the quietest visits, arrive at opening (usually 09:00) on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday. Most sights are busiest between 11:00 and 15:00 and on weekends. Check closing days first: Monday (Dolmabahçe, Beylerbeyi, Rumeli Fortress, Miniatürk), Tuesday (Topkapı), Wednesday (Yıldız), Friday (Chora; mosques at midday) and Sunday (Grand Bazaar).

Not: Kısa cevap tamamen veri dosyasından üretiliyor; kapalı gün listesi 20 yerin resmi kapalı günleriyle birebir aynı. Eski yazıdaki 'Topkapı Çarşamba en sakin / Çarşamba taşma' çelişkisi yok: Topkapı'da Salı kapanışından sonraki gün (Çarşamba) hafif yoğun varsayıldı ve tablo da aynı şeyi gösteriyor (en sakin Perşembe).

Which month is the busiest in Istanbul?

July and October were the busiest months in 2025, with about 1.9 million foreign visitors each. March was the quietest, with 1.23 million, about 35% fewer than July. The calendar above shows peak season; from November to March expect noticeably calmer sights.

A quiet plan for each day of the week

  • Monday: Sultanahmet day. Topkapı Palace at 09:00, then the Archaeological Museums next door. The Bosphorus palaces and Miniatürk are closed.
  • Tuesday: Topkapı is closed, so start at Hagia Sophia at 09:00, then the Basilica Cistern and the Blue Mosque before noon. Dolmabahçe in the afternoon.
  • Wednesday: Dolmabahçe at 09:00, then Ortaköy and Rumeli Fortress along the Bosphorus. Yıldız Palace is closed.
  • Thursday: The calmest day at Topkapı. Go at opening, see the Harem first, then the Turkish and Islamic Arts Museum.
  • Friday: Avoid mosques between about 12:00 and 14:30. Galata Tower in the morning, Maiden's Tower and the palaces. Chora is closed. The pass's guided tours and hosted entries at Hagia Sophia, the Basilica Cistern, Galata Tower and the Blue Mosque do not run on Fridays.
  • Saturday: The busiest day almost everywhere. Use the early hours for your top sight and keep the afternoon for Beylerbeyi or a ferry.
  • Sunday: The Grand Bazaar is closed; the Spice Bazaar is open but busiest after 11:00. Palaces and museums open as usual.

Frequently asked questions

What is the least crowded day to visit Istanbul museums?

Tuesday to Thursday mornings are the calmest overall. Check weekly closing days first: Topkapı Palace closes on Tuesday, and several Bosphorus palaces close on Monday.

What time should I arrive to avoid crowds?

Arrive at opening time, usually 09:00. Most sights are busiest between 11:00 and 15:00; Galata Tower is busiest in the hour before sunset.

Which Istanbul museums are closed on Monday?

Dolmabahçe Palace, Beylerbeyi Palace, Rumeli Fortress and Miniatürk are closed on Mondays.

Which Istanbul museums are closed on Tuesday?

Topkapı Palace is closed on Tuesdays. Hagia Sophia, the Basilica Cistern and the Istanbul Archaeological Museums stay open.

Is Hagia Sophia open on Fridays?

Yes, but the visitor gallery closes from 30 minutes before until 90 minutes after the Friday noon prayer, roughly 12:20–14:30 at this time of year.

Is the Grand Bazaar open on Sunday?

No. The Grand Bazaar is closed on Sundays and religious holidays. The Spice Bazaar is open every day.

Which month is the busiest to visit Istanbul?

In 2025, July had the most foreign visitors (1.88 million), closely followed by October. March was the quietest month, with about 35% fewer visitors than July.

Are these live crowd levels?

No. They are estimates for a typical week, based on official opening hours and closing days and each sight's usual pattern. Opening hours and closing days come from official sources where they exist.
